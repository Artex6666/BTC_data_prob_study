## 1. THE CORE STRATEGY: MAKER CHASER (THE SNAPSHOT)
The strategy is a "Marker Chaser" that uses the Binance Spot price as a lead indicator to front-run the slower Polymarket order book.

- **Trigger (Floor)**: The bot remains dormant until the Market Bid (Polymarket) reaches a threshold (default **90¢** for BTC/ETH). 
- **Activation Area**: Sniping exclusively in the "Late Game" (typically the final **60 seconds** of M5/M15/H1 contracts).
- **Chase Logic**: Once triggered, the bot places a Limit Bid at top-of-book and "chases" every tick up to **99¢**.
- **The Alpha**: The bot captures "Settlement Arbitrage" volume—fills that occur at 99¢ in the final seconds (or even milliseconds after expiration) from sellers who value instant liquidity over waiting for Chainlink resolution.

This is the most critical technical requirement for the Rust implementation.
